New feature ideas for Nextzen Orbit

Every idea here serves the app's goal: helping job seekers in India get hired faster with AI tools plus a done-for-you team that applies for them. None of them repeat what the app already does (resumes, analyzer, cover letters, tracker, job search, autofill, career roadmaps, interview question bank, admin apply queue). Instead, each one builds on those pieces.

S about 1–3 days M about 1–2 weeks L 3+ weeks ★ recommended first

Start here: the top 5

These are the most useful for the least effort, given what's already built.

  1. Client progress feed: paying clients can see the work the team does for them.
  2. Saved searches and job alerts: gives people a reason to come back every day.
  3. AI mock interviews: puts the interview question bank to active use.
  4. Which resume gets callbacks: ties tracker outcomes to resume versions.
  5. CTC to in-hand salary and offer comparison: a common, India-specific pain point that's cheap to build.

1 · Done-for-you service transparency

Admins already apply on clients' behalf, but clients can't see that work. Showing it is what justifies the subscription.

Client progress feed S

★ top pick

A dashboard timeline: "Your team applied to 14 roles this week, tailored 3 resumes, and 2 are in interview." Plus a weekly email summary.

Why: it cuts churn and support questions like "what am I paying for?"

Reuse: admin apply queue, applications, admin document workspace jobs, /api/notifications

Client ↔ team messaging M

Threaded chat for each client with their assigned admins. Clients can approve or reject a target job before it's applied to, and share preferences like "no service companies".

Why: replaces WhatsApp side channels and keeps context with the account.

Reuse: shared client assignments (migration 039), Supabase Realtime

Approve-before-apply targets S

A client gets a daily shortlist of roles the team plans to apply to and can swipe yes or no on each.

Why: fewer wasted applications and higher client trust.

Reuse: /api/admin/targets, application targets

WhatsApp notifications M

Opt-in WhatsApp messages for interview calls, status changes and weekly summaries. Email is often ignored in India; WhatsApp isn't.

Why: much higher open rates than email.

Reuse: notifications pipeline; needs a WhatsApp Business provider

2 · Getting more callbacks

Saved searches and job alerts M

★ top pick

Save a search (role, city, experience, salary) and get a daily digest of new matches, each with a fit score from the analyzer.

Why: a daily reason to return, which the app currently lacks.

Reuse: jobs-service, Adzuna search, analyzer, a Vercel cron route

Which resume gets callbacks S

★ top pick

Records which resume version each application used, then shows the response rate per version. A simple form of A/B testing for resumes.

Why: turns the tracker from a log into a feedback loop.

Reuse: resume_versions, applications

LinkedIn profile optimizer S

Generates a headline, About section and skills list from the master resume and target role, with copy buttons for each.

Why: recruiters in India source heavily from LinkedIn.

Reuse: AI prompts in src/lib/ai (now Bedrock), resume data

Referral and networking CRM M

Store contacts per company (alumni, ex-colleagues), generate outreach messages and referral requests, and set follow-up reminders.

Why: referrals convert far better than cold applications.

Reuse: applications (link a contact to a company), notes

Follow-up reminders S

"Applied 7 days ago, no response": prompts the user with a ready-made follow-up email to the recruiter.

Reuse: application dates, cover letter generator

Skill-gap to roadmap link S

When the analyzer finds missing skills, offer one click to add the matching career-roadmap steps and YouTube lessons.

Reuse: analyzer, roadmaps-service, youtube-service

3 · Interviews and offers

AI mock interviews M

★ top pick

Timed practice rounds for a specific job description, answered by text or voice. The AI scores each answer (structure, STAR method, relevance) and suggests a stronger version.

Reuse: interview-service, interview question bank, Bedrock AI

Company prep brief S

A one-page brief for each interview: what the company does, the likely interview rounds, questions to ask, and how to frame your experience for this role.

Reuse: applications, AI notes

CTC to in-hand and offer comparison S

★ top pick

Breaks a CTC into fixed pay, variable pay, PF, gratuity, tax regime and monthly in-hand. Compares 2–3 offers side by side, including joining bonus and notice-period buyout.

Why: a common confusion point in India; it's pure calculation, so there's no AI cost.

Negotiation and notice-period helper S

Counter-offer scripts, early-release requests and resignation letter templates, all personalised.

Reuse: cover letter generator and export

Interview calendar sync M

Interview dates from the tracker sync to Google Calendar, with reminders and the prep brief attached.

Reuse: Google sign-in already exists; needs the calendar scope

4 · Growth and retention

Public profile page M

nextzenorbit.com/u/name: a shareable portfolio built from the resume and projects. Each page also works as free SEO for the marketing site.

Reuse: projects-service, resume templates; works well with the domain split

Refer-a-friend credits S

An invite link that gives both people a discount or extra days on their plan.

Reuse: subscriptions, PayU checkout

Document vault for background checks S

Secure storage for offer letters, relieving letters, payslips and certificates, ready to share during BGV (background verification).

Reuse: Supabase Storage plus the existing file routes and RLS

Fresher and campus mode M

Onboarding, resume templates and roadmaps tuned for graduates with no experience: projects and internships first, plus aptitude-prep links.

Reuse: onboarding, templates, careers

5 · Admin and team efficiency

Workload and SLA board S

Shows applications per admin per day, clients with no activity in 3+ days, and approvals waiting on review.

Reuse: admin analytics, assignments

Quality review queue M

A supervisor admin samples generated resumes and cover letters before they're sent, with a checklist and a score for each admin.

Reuse: supervisor_admin role, document workspace

Bulk tailor and apply L

Select 10 target jobs, generate tailored resumes for all of them as background jobs, review them, then work through the queue with the autofill extension.

Reuse: document jobs (migration 041), autofill extension
